Apollo Download Manager extension

Links go to the desktop app through the browser's native messaging. Cookies and logins from the browser are not sent, so a download that needs them may fail in the app; then download it in the browser.

Capture

When the browser starts a download

Filters

Torrents

Off by default. Magnet clicks need access to pages, which the browser asks you for. The app always asks before adding a torrent.

Privacy

The popup keeps your last 5 sends, the site and file name only, in this browser. Nothing leaves your computer except the links you send to the app.
